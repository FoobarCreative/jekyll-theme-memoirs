Stezza is a simple, stylish music player for your Apple Music and iTunes library. Built for people who just want to play their music — no clutter, no distractions.

* Stream from Apple Music and iTunes Match
* Play your synced music library
* Video playback for files copied via Finder or iTunes
* Clean, distraction-free interface

"Stezza is meant to be quick, easy and accessible. If that's all you're after, look no further." — Cult of Mac
