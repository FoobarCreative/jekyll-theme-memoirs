A safe web browser built just for kids. Parents can approve websites, and external links are blocked.

Wincy puts parents in control with powerful features designed for peace of mind:
* Parent-approved website filtering
* Customizable permissions
* Built-in parental controls
* Curated, age-appropriate search results
* Screen time management with scheduling
* Educational resource access
* Child-friendly interface

Whether your kids are learning, exploring, or having fun online, Wincy keeps them safe in a controlled, age-appropriate browsing environment.

Built for iOS with safety and simplicity in mind from Adelaide, Australia.
