Transform your home with just a snap and a tap. Renosnap uses AI to reimagine your spaces — try new styles, colors, and layouts before you commit to a single change.

Snap a photo of any room and explore endless design possibilities:
* AI-powered room redesigns
* Multiple style options at your fingertips
* Visualize before you renovate
* Quick, photo-based workflow
* Inspiration for any space

Whether you're planning a full renovation or just want to refresh a single room, Renosnap helps you see what's possible.

Built for iOS from Adelaide, Australia.
